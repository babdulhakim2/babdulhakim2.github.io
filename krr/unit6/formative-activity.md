# Unit 6 Formative Activity

## Task
Read Ciroku et al (2024) and conduct research on different ontology development strategies. Reflect on benefits, drawbacks, and suitable scenarios for each approach.

## Ontology Development Strategies

**Top-Down:** Systematic but time-consuming. Best for well-structured domains like medical diagnosis.

**Bottom-Up:** Data-driven but can lack structure. Good for dynamic domains like social media.

**Middle-Out:** Balanced approach focusing on core concepts. Suitable for enterprise systems.

**Reverse Engineering (Ciroku et al):** AI-driven extraction from existing knowledge graphs. Useful for legacy system integration.

## My Analysis

What struck me about Ciroku et al's approach is how AI can now reverse-engineer the very questions that should guide ontology design. It's like having the computer tell us what we should have been asking all along. The idea of using language models to extract competency questions from existing knowledge graphs flips traditional ontology development on its head - instead of starting with human expertise, we're mining implicit knowledge that's already been captured. Makes me wonder if this could bridge the gap between messy real-world data and the clean theoretical models we usually aim for.
