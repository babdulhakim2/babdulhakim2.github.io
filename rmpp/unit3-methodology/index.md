# e-Portfolio Activity: Research Proposal Review

### Which research methods would suit your purpose?

**Mixed Methods approach** - quantitative analysis of fraud detection performance metrics combined with qualitative interviews capturing implementation challenges in financial institutions.

### Which data collection methods would you consider?

**Primary:** Semi-structured interviews with fraud analysts, surveys of financial crime professionals, case study analysis
**Secondary:** Academic databases (IEEE Xplore, ACM), regulatory reports (FCA, PSR), industry documentation

### Which skills will you need to develop?

**Technical Skills:** Statistical analysis, interview design, survey construction
**Existing Strengths:** Engineering background, financial crime expertise, machine learning knowledge, anti-money laundering systems experience

## References

Saunders, M., Lewis, P. and Thornhill, A. (2012) *Research Methods for Business Students*. 6th edn. Pearson Education Limited.

Dawson, C. (2015) *Projects in Computing and Information Systems: A Student's Guide*. Harlow: Pearson.
